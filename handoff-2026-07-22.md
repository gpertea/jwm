## The work (see CLAUDE.md "main feature" for full statements)

Per-screen trays/taskbars so tint2 + wmctrl duct tape can be removed:

1. `<Tray screen="N" valign="top|bottom">` - screen-bound trays,
   position/width computed from that screen's geometry (handles the
   vertical monitor and the home stacked-monitor layout).
2. TaskList filtered to windows with dominant surface on the tray's
   screen (tint2 semantics).
3. Per-screen bar height, 2-3 task-button rows on the vertical
   monitor's bar (TaskList already has some multi-row ability -
   verify/extend rather than rebuild).
4. Per-screen usable-area/strut handling: a bar only shrinks ITS
   screen's maximize/placement bounds (place.c, client.c).
5. Better task icon handling (later in the main track).

Minor roadmap after that (CLAUDE.md): task-button drag reorder,
task-button right-click menu (works while minimized), window rename,
alt-tab polish, thumbnails (luxury).
