## Testing notes (hard-won, do not rediscover)

- VNC session on :1 runs its own `jwm -f ~/.jwmrc-vnc` - never pkill
  by bare "jwm" pattern.
- Xephyr dead space (outside Xinerama screens) keeps stale framebuffer
  garbage that looks like duplicated bars; xsetroot -solid before
  screenshots. Verify geometry with `xwininfo -root -tree`.
- Drive WM-mediated moves with `wmctrl -i -r <id> -e 0,x,y,-1,-1`
  (ids from wmctrl -l). xdotool windowmove bypasses the WM (moves the
  inner widget within the frame) - useless for testing migration.
- xterm titles get overwritten by shell prompts; use
  `xterm -T NAME -e sleep 1000` for stable labels in screenshots.
